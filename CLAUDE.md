# Shreya & Akshay — Save the Date

A mobile-first, heavily animated save-the-date website. **Its only job is to collect a formal RSVP.** The full invitation card follows separately, so don't add schedules, venue maps, FAQs, registries or anything else unless the user asks.

## Fixed facts (do not change without the user)
- Couple: **Shreya & Akshay** (first names). Tone: "We're getting married", **with both family names shown** (family names: _ask the user if they're not filled in here_).
- Dates: **16 & 17 February 2027** (two days).
- Place: **Jim Corbett, Uttarakhand**. Show the **region only**; the exact venue is revealed later in the formal card. Never put the venue name in copy, metadata or the share image.
- Audience: ~200–220 guests, shared as **one link on WhatsApp**. Design for a phone in portrait first; desktop is secondary.
- Launch deadline: **13–14 Nov 2026** (2–3 days from 11 Nov 2026). Scope must fit that.

## RSVP
- Fields: name (required), attending yes/no (required), number of guests (shown only when attending), phone (optional), email (optional). RSVP by 24 November 2026. No grandparents' blessing line for now (`blessingsLine: ''` in `src/content.ts`).
- Storage: Google Sheet via Apps Script. Server: `apps-script/Code.gs`. Client: `src/rsvp.ts` → `sendRsvp()`. Endpoint URL lives in `.env.local` as `VITE_RSVP_URL` (template: `.env.example`, never commit the real one).
- Requests are sent as `text/plain` JSON to avoid a CORS preflight. Keep it that way.
- **Personalised links later:** design so `?g=<guestId>` can pre-fill a name and cap the guest count. Don't build it until there's a guest list; just don't block it (e.g. keep the greeting and the guest-count max as props, not hard-coded).

## Must-haves
- **Add to calendar:** Google Calendar link + downloadable `.ics` for both days.
- **WhatsApp link preview:** Open Graph title, description and a 1200×630 `og:image`. This is the first thing every guest sees.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: every animation has a calm fallback, and the RSVP is reachable without completing the animated intro.
- Performance on mid-range Android over 4G: lazy-load 3D/Lottie, compress illustrations (WebP/AVIF, SVG where possible), target < 2 MB first load.
- Accessible form: labels, visible focus, clear errors, success state.

## Explicitly out (user decisions)
- No wedding hashtag. No companion video (it would pull people away from the site). No music for now (revisit once the design is final; audio would only start after a tap).

## Open decisions
- **Benchmark the user gave:** missingpieceinvites.com "Mountain" (demo: /demos/mountain). It's a layered paper-diorama look: textured velvet and handmade paper, gold foil, torn-paper arches, die-cut sticker objects, painterly folk/miniature-style couple art, Indian invitation grammar (Ganesha, mantra, blessings, "daughter of / son of"), and scroll-driven parallax depth. Its quality comes from **art assets** (generated or painted), not from shapes drawn in code. Match that level.
- Visual direction. **Cute or code-drawn illustration is rejected** (the crayon/storybook moodboard in `docs/moodboard.html` was "like a kid with crayons"). Candidates live in `docs/directions.html`: 1 Type, 2 Photos cinematic, 3 Object (envelope + wax seal), 4 Light & Glass, 5 Names made of light. Mostly pastels; craft must read as a senior designer's work. The childhood-friends story can stay in via real then/now photo pairs.
- Family names. Domain (user will buy one later). Song.

## Art pipeline (how the hero was made; reuse it for every new scene)
- Art is generated in Figma AI (file "Shreya & Akshay – Save the Date Art", key `sHhkMiXFzTYPWYqrutCFKr`; each call costs Figma credits, so ask before generating). Style tokens to keep consistent: "refined Indian Pahari miniature gouache on textured handmade cotton paper, torn deckled edges, fine gold-foil accents, pastel blush / peach / powder blue / dusty lilac / sage / antique gold, no text".
- Each depth layer is generated on flat magenta `#FF00FF`, raw PNG saved to `art-src/<scene>/`, then `node scripts/key-magenta.mjs in.png public/<scene>/out.webp [--spill=0] [--saturation=0.62] [--opaque]` keys it to a transparent WebP.
- Hero (`src/Hero.tsx`, `src/hero.css`): sky → hills → forest → fireflies → grass + tiger → velvet arch frame. Intro = paper layers stand up like a pop-up book; scroll (pinned, scrubbed) = camera walks through the arch, a hornbill flies across, the date card arrives.
- The Browser pane throttles animation when hidden. Use `window.heroIntro` and `ScrollTrigger` (exposed in dev) to drive states, and trust computed styles over screenshots.

## Page structure
- `src/Story.tsx` + `src/story.css`: **one pinned stage** (portrait column, `width: min(100%, 62svh)`, `container-type: inline-size` so card type scales in `cqw`) and **one scrubbed GSAP timeline measured in units `U = 7`**. 0–1 is the gate (pop-up intro, then through the arch); 1–3 the journey (trail, jeep in/out, deer bound off, families card); 3–5.4 the venue (camera pans, curtain drops, date card + calendar); 5.4–7 dusk (the same venue layers duplicated as `.s4` with a multiply `.dusk` overlay, diyas, fireflies, parakeets) and the RSVP card. The pin ends on the RSVP, which only becomes clickable at the end (`.rsvp-live`).
- The hornbill guide runs on a rAF loop: `STOPS` maps the timeline unit to a perch (`[data-perch]` elements on the arch, jeep, date card and RSVP card) or an open-sky waypoint; it faces the scroll direction; poses are `.fly-pose` (flapping pieces) and `.perch-pose`.
- `src/RsvpCard.tsx`: the form; `onAccepted` triggers the marigold petal shower (`shower()` in Story). A sent response is remembered in localStorage.
- `src/App.tsx`: Story plus the floating RSVP button (jumps to `ScrollTrigger.getById('story').end`).
- Reduced motion: no pin; the four scenes stack as still 100svh panels (CSS at the bottom of `story.css`).
- Dev only: `window.storyIntro`, `gsap` and `ScrollTrigger` are exposed so the preview can be driven (`storyIntro.progress(1)`, then scroll plus `ScrollTrigger.getById('story').animation.progress(u / 7)`). The preview pane paints late, so take two screenshots or trust computed styles.
- **All guest-facing copy lives in `src/content.ts`.**
- Don't name a component `Rsvp.tsx`: on macOS it clashes with `rsvp.ts`.
