The hornbill guide

  1. The existing hornbill is cut into a body and two wings, which flap from the shoulders while it flies. No new art is needed.
  2. Each scene has a perch on one of its motifs: the arch tip, the jeep's garlanded bonnet, the venue's floral arch, the top of the RSVP card.
  3. Scroll down and it flies forward, the same way as the jeep. Scroll up and it turns round and flies back to the previous perch.
  4. When it lands, the wings fold in. One new image, a matching perched hornbill, makes the landing pose perfect.

The journey

  1. The hero's forest melts into the trail painting while the camera keeps moving, so the two forests blend.
  2. The hornbill lands on the bonnet's marigold garland as your jeep drives in from the right, with wheel bounce and a dust puff, and eases to a stop.
  3. The paper card rises: "Together with our families · We, Shreya & Akshay, are getting married", then Shreya, daughter of Dr. Anju Garg & Dr. Rakesh Garg, and Akshay, son of Mrs. Mamta Jain & Mr. Amit Jain.

  5. The hornbill takes off and the jeep follows it out to the left. The flower border drifts faster than the trail.

Arrival at the venue

  1. The camera follows the jeep round the bend. The trail slides away left and your venue opens up.
  2. The flower curtain drops from the top like a toran and sways once.
  3. Gold glints run along the river, and the camera drifts across the lawn to the villas. The hornbill settles on the floral arch.
  4. A pair of kingfishers sits on a branch over the water, a couple of their own. They bob and turn to look at each other as you scroll.
  5. A peacock steps across the lawn and, at the card's arrival, lifts and fans its tail once.
  6. The card arrives with the dates and Add to Calendar. The venue is shown but never named.

Dusk in the garden

  1. The sky over your venue warms to a lavender-and-peach dusk. Diyas light up one by one along the garden path, and the first fireflies rise.
  2. The RSVP card rises under the flower curtain and the hornbill perches on its top corner: name, joyfully accept or regretfully decline, guests, then phone and email (both optional).
  3. The kingfishers are still on their branch, now in the dusk light, so the place carries on unchanged. A small flock of parakeets flies home across the sky.
  4. On accept, marigold petals fall and the diyas glow brighter: "We can't wait to celebrate with you."
  5. The story closes on S & A in the dusk light.
