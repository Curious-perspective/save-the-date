Concept A · childhood friends to married couple

Passing Notes

It started with notes passed under the desk. The save-the-date is the last one: a folded chit that guests unfold and tick.

Story as you scroll

  1. A folded chit lands on a ruled notebook page. Tap to unfold it; the paper creases open in 3D.
  2. Margin doodles grow up as you scroll: two kids with school bags, then bicycles, then exam-hall nerves, then the two of you, drawn from your photos.
  3. The dates sit under a crayon scratch card, like the ones from the school canteen. Scratch to reveal 16 & 17 Feb.
  4. "Mark your calendar" is a school timetable. One tap adds both days to Google or Apple Calendar.
  5. The RSVP is the chit itself: name, tick Yes or No, number of guests, phone. On send it folds into a paper plane and flies off.

Concept B · Jim Corbett, romantic and botanical

Permit to the Wild

Every guest gets an entry permit to the jungle where you say yes. The page is a walk from dawn mist to the two of you under a sal tree.

Story as you scroll

  1. A safari-style entry permit with the guest's name on it (generic now, personalised once you have the list). Tap and the forest gate swings open.
  2. Dawn forest in layers that shift as the guest tilts their phone: sal trees, ferns, drifting mist, fireflies.
  3. Pugmarks lead down the page. A tiger peeks from the grass, a hornbill crosses, leaves part to reveal your names.
  4. The two of you, illustrated from your photos, beside the Ramganga river. The dates are on a wooden trail sign, with an Add to Calendar button.
  5. RSVP form on the permit. On send, a rubber stamp thuds down: "Entry confirmed".
