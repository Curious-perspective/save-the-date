Steps 2–6

From here to sending it

Pick the opening moment

The first three seconds after a guest taps the link in WhatsApp. One of these, matched to the direction:

Break the sealThumb on the wax, it cracks, the envelope opens. Works with 3.
Fly through your initialsThe monogram becomes a window into your photo. Works with 2.
Names gather from lightParticles assemble into your names. Works with 5.
Curtain of typeLetters rise from behind a line, one by one. Works with 1.
Press and hold to openA ring fills under the thumb, then the page blooms open. Works with any.

Shape the scroll

One idea per screen, in this order. Dashed items are optional; the RSVP stays reachable from the first screen through a small fixed button.

Drag · stand-ins for two real photos

This is how the childhood-friends story can stay in, with no drawings. A childhood photo of the two of you and a recent one in the same pose. Guests drag between them. If you have two or three such pairs, this becomes the emotional centre of the page.

Make the RSVP feel like part of the design

Only what you asked for. Number of guests appears once they tap Yes. After sending, the opening moment plays once more as the thank-you, and the row lands in your Google Sheet.
